Background and approach

About

I work at the intersection of electrocatalysis, inorganic materials and data-driven scientific research.

My research interests span electrochemical reaction pathways, structure–property relationships and the integration of experiments, characterization and computational methods. I am particularly interested in using reliable evidence and automated workflows to make materials research more efficient and reproducible.

Areas of interest

Research interests

Three complementary directions connecting chemical mechanisms, inorganic materials and AI-assisted discovery.

01

Electrochemical conversion

Catalyst design, reaction selectivity and electrochemical upgrading of small molecules and carbon-containing feedstocks.

02

Reaction mechanisms & materials characterization

Connecting measurable electrochemical performance to active states, oxygen pathways and material structure using complementary experimental evidence.

03

AI-assisted materials discovery

Closed-loop scientific workflows, research automation and data-guided experimental decisions for inorganic materials research.
