01 / About

About

My work connects electrochemical catalysis, inorganic materials and AI-assisted scientific research. I am interested in how atomic-scale mechanisms shape materials performance, and how experiments, characterization and computation can be integrated to accelerate discovery.

02 / Research

Research interests

Selected directions at the interface of materials chemistry, electrocatalysis and scientific automation.

01 / ELECTROCATALYSIS

Electrochemical conversion

Catalyst design, reaction selectivity and electrochemical upgrading of small molecules, including carbon-containing feedstocks.

02 / MECHANISMS

Reaction pathways & characterization

Mechanistic investigations using electrochemical methods, materials characterization and isotope-based evidence.

03 / AI FOR SCIENCE

Data-driven materials discovery

Research automation, computational workflows and data-informed experimental planning for inorganic materials.
