RESEARCH · ELECTROCHEMISTRY · AI FOR SCIENCE

Fanxu Meng

I am a Research Fellow at the Berkeley Education Alliance for Research in Singapore (BEARS), working on mechanistic electrochemistry, evidence-grounded AI and physical-AI-enabled electrochemical discovery.

My research investigates hidden electrochemical states, connecting experimental observations to mechanisms and translating scientific evidence into reliable experimental decisions. Current efforts integrate electrochemical measurements, isotope-based mechanistic analysis, multiphysics simulation, AI-guided experiment selection and laboratory automation.

I received my PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) under Prof Zhichuan J. Xu and currently work with Assoc Prof Kedar Hippalgaonkar.

Research

Research Interests

Mechanistic electrochemistry

Catalyst oxygen migration, lattice-oxygen participation, isotope-labelling measurements, product selectivity and reaction pathways.

Evidence-grounded AI for scientific inference

Reproducible scientific analysis and experiment-design workflows linking native records, quality control, uncertainty and competing mechanistic hypotheses.

AI-guided electrochemical discovery

Physics-informed electrochemical state detection, active learning, Bayesian optimization and multicomponent electrocatalyst exploration.

Physical AI and laboratory automation

Traceable experimental execution, electrochemical measurements, automated MEA construction and device-scale validation.
